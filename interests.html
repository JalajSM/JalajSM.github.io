ML for Materials Science

Materials science is drowning in high-dimensional data: phase diagrams, composition spaces, spectra, microscopy stacks. Most of it still gets analyzed by hand or with narrow purpose-built tools. Surrogate modeling and physics-informed ML are the interesting part of the frontier.

Law & Policy

How science gets funded and turned into policy matters as much as the science itself, especially on climate. Unfortunately, most of the actual debates happen far from the labs producing the underlying research, and I believe that needs to change.

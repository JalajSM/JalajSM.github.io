ML for Materials Science

Materials science is drowning in high-dimensional data — phase diagrams, composition spaces, spectra, microscopy stacks — and most of it still gets analyzed by hand or with narrow, purpose-built tools. Surrogate modeling, active learning, and physics-informed ML are the interesting frontier, and where a lot of my recent side work has been focused.

⚖

Law & Policy

How science gets funded, regulated, and translated into practice matters as much as the science itself, especially on climate and environmental questions. I read and think a lot about the intersection of research, regulation, and environmental outcomes — and some of that ends up in my writing.
